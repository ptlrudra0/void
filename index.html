Extra check on unseen phishing

Read this

  • Models use only the text of the URL (29 features such as length, dots, hyphens, digits, entropy, @, IP, https, suspicious words). They do not open the page.
  • The legitimate URLs are links collected from the homepages of popular sites (Tranco list), so the data is not a perfect picture of the real web. Real-world accuracy can be lower.
  • Models: Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, linear SVM, XGBoost, small neural net (MLP). All trained with scikit-learn / XGBoost and exported to run in JavaScript.
  • Trusted-domain rule layer (on top of the ML models): exact matches on a short list of well-known domains and CHARUSAT sites (charusat.ac.in, charusat.edu.in incl. e-Governance, charusat.online) are not flagged. Lookalikes such as charusat.ac.in.evil.com or charusat.ac.in@evil.com are not trusted and go through the ML models. Shared hosts like sites.google.com or github.io are never trusted by this rule.
  • The verdict above is the average probability of the 6 models (50% cut-off). Treat it as a demo, not a security product.
